  # Step 04 — Update Merchant: Business Details


  Adds business category, sub-category, expected volume, GST, business name, and
  CIN (for eligible entities).


  ## Prerequisite Steps

  - Step 02 (PAN + Entity) — entity type must be set

  - Step 03 (CKYC) — should be attempted before this step


  ## Entity Applicability

  **All entities.** CIN is conditional:

  - `cin_number` required for: **Pvt Ltd, Public Limited, One Person Company**

  - `cin_number` NOT required for: Individual, Sole Prop, Partnership, LLP,
  Trust, Society

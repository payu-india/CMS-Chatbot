  # Step 06 — Upload Bank Statement


  Uploads bank statements (6-12 months) for POS merchant risk assessment.


  ## Prerequisite Steps

  - Step 05 (Bank Details) — bank details must be added first


  ## Applicability

  **Mandatory for POS Merchants** (`pos_merchant=true`).

  Non-POS merchants skip this step.


  ## Purpose

  Bank statements are required for POS merchants to assess financial risk and
  determine POS device eligibility.

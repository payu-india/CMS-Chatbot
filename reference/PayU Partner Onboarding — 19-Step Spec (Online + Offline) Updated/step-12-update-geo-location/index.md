  # Step 12 — Update: Geo-Location


  Captures the merchant's physical location coordinates for risk verification.


  ## Prerequisite Steps

  - Step 11 (Addresses) — addresses should be set first


  ## Applicability

  Applicable for offline merchants. Online merchants may skip this step.


  ## Purpose

  Geo-location is captured during agent/field visits for offline merchants to
  verify physical presence at the declared address.

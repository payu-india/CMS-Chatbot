  Adds the authorised signatory who will sign the merchant agreement (Step 16).


  ## Prerequisite Steps

  - Step 07 (Website Details)


  ## Entity Applicability

  **All entities**


  ## Important


  - This step is a **prerequisite for DigiLocker** (Step 09) — DigiLocker cannot
  succeed without signatory details

  - The **CIN field** in signatory details is **only for CIN-eligible
  entities**: Pvt Ltd, Public Limited, One Person Company

  - Do NOT send CIN in signatory details for other entity types

This step is to submit UBO details. UBOs are individuals who own or control 25%+ of the business.

## Prerequisite 

Steps- Step 11 (VKYC)

## Entity Applicability

**ONLY for:** Private Limited, Public Limited, Partnership, Trust, LLP, Society

**NOT required for:** Individual, Sole Proprietorship, One Person Company, Government, NGO, Hindu Undivided Family
Multiple UBOs can be submitted using array indexing: `ubo[0]`, `ubo[1]`, etc.

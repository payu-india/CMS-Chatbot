Fetch the offers available for a merchant, or validate one offer before payment.

* [Fetch offers API](ref:partner-fetch-offer-api) — Return offers for a merchant. Amount, offer keys, payment id, user token, and SKU details are optional.
* [Validate offer API](ref:partner-validate-offer-api) — Check an offer against the payment category and the customer.

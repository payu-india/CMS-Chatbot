  ## Overview


  This is the last step to complete the onboarding of your merchants in PayU.
  Use the Esign APIs to,


  1. Generate the agreement for signing.
      
  2. Send an OTP to the registered signatory email of the merchant.
      
  3. Validate the OTP & complete the Esign process.
      

  ## Prerequisites


  Ensure that the statuses for the following statges are verified before
  proceeding for Agreement Signing:


  1. Bank details
      
  2. Website details
      
  3. KYC details
      
  4. Video KYC details
      
  5. Business Members Details

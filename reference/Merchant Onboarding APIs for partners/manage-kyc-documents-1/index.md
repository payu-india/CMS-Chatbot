  # Overview


  After updating the required business details of your merchant, you need to
  upload few documents of the merchant as a part of the KYC (Know Your
  Customer). These APIs let's you:


  1. Fetch the list of documents required for the merchant.
      
  2. Upload the docs one at a time.
      
  3. You can also delete a KYC doc.

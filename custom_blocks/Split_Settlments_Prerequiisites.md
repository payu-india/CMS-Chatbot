name: Split_Settlments_Prerequiisites
---
Before you start, ensure the following:

1. Your parent merchant account is activated for Split Settlements.
2. Child merchants have completed onboarding process and are marked Completed/Active .
3. You have your parent merchant `key` and `salt`, along with the child merchant(s) key.
4. You have server-side logic to generate hash and process webhooks/You have the `payuId` of the already completed parent transaction.

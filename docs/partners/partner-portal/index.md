PayU Partner Portal helps to grow your business by onboarding your merchants and tracking your incentives. Your merchants can use the various payment methods to collect payments from their customers.

With Partner Portal, you can:

* Refer Merchants​
* Complete the Merchant’s profile on behalf of the merchant​
* Track their referred merchant’s onboarding status​
* Provide a co-branded onboarding experience for the merchant via OAuth (no FE development from partner)​
* Track Incentives earned on referrals​
* Get Settlement and other reports for merchants

After you onboard the merchants, they can start collecting payments from their customers. For more information, refer to [Web Integration](doc:introduction-web).

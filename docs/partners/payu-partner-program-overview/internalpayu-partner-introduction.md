## What is the PayU Partner Program?

Think of it like becoming a **PayU reseller or referral agent**. If you know businesses that need to accept online payments, you can:

✅ **Refer them to PayU** and help them get started  
✅ **Earn money (incentives)** for each successful merchant you bring  
✅ **Offer PayU's payment solutions** under your own brand  

**Perfect for:**
- Web designers and developers
- Digital marketing agencies
- E-commerce platform providers (like Shopify, WooCommerce)
- Business consultants
- Anyone who works with businesses that need online payment solutions

## How Does It Work? (4 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: 📝 **Register as a Partner**
Sign up at [partner.payu.in](https://partner.payu.in) and complete your KYC (identity verification).

### Step 2: 🎯 **Refer Merchants**
Send businesses to PayU using one of these easy methods:
- **Simple referral link** - just share a link
- **Partner Portal** - add merchant details yourself
- **Co-branded signup page** - merchants sign up on a page with YOUR branding
- **API integration** - for tech-savvy partners who want full automation

### Step 3: ✅ **Merchant Gets Onboarded**
The business completes their profile and verification (either by themselves or you can help them). PayU handles all the background checks automatically.

### Step 4: 💵 **You Get Paid**
Once the merchant starts processing payments, you earn your incentives! Payments are settled monthly.

## Types of Integration (Choose Your Style)

### 🔗 **Option 1: Referral Links** (Easiest)
**Best for:** Beginners, small-scale referrals  
**How it works:** Just share a special link. When businesses sign up through your link, they're automatically tagged as your referral.

### 🖥️ **Option 2: Partner Portal** (User-Friendly)
**Best for:** Partners who want hands-on control  
**How it works:** Log into the portal and manually add merchant details or upload multiple merchants via Excel.

### 🎨 **Option 3: Co-Branded OAuth** (Professional)
**Best for:** Agencies and platforms with their own website  
**How it works:** Merchants sign up on a page that looks like YOUR website but connects to PayU in the background.

### ⚙️ **Option 4: API Integration** (Advanced)
**Best for:** Tech companies and platform providers  
**How it works:** Full programmatic control - automatically onboard merchants from your own system.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need technical knowledge to become a partner?**  
A: Not necessarily! If you're just referring merchants via links or the portal, no coding required. API integration needs technical knowledge.

**Q: How much can I earn?**  
A: It depends on your pricing agreement with PayU. You'll earn incentives based on the merchants you refer and their transaction volumes.

**Q: Can I offer this under my own brand name?**  
A: Yes! You can use co-branded onboarding where merchants see your logo and branding.

**Q: What if my referred merchant needs support?**  
A: PayU provides full merchant support. You can also track any issues through your partner dashboard.

**Q: How long does merchant onboarding take?**  
A: With automatic KYC verification, merchants can get onboarded in as little as 24-48 hours if all documents are in order.

**Q: Is there a minimum number of merchants I need to refer?**  
A: No minimum! Start small and scale as you grow.

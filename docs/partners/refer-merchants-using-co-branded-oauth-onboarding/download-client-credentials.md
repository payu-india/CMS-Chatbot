To view and download the client credentials on your Partner Dashboard:

> 📘 Note:
>
> The **My App** menu option (under the drop-down menu) mentioned in this procedure is not enabled by default. Contact your PayU Key Account Manager to enable the **My App** menu option.

1. Log on to Partner Dashboard.
2. Click your profile picture on the top-right corner and select **My App** from the drop-down menu.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/b3e8c99125068fda5648febbd3ccbb08d8a22074d8170d447e5f264d10596dfe-Screenshot_2025-08-26_at_2.45.17_PM.png" />

<Image align="center" width="422px" src="https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/03/Screenshot-2022-03-31-at-5.16.27-PM-1.png" />

3. Expand the **Download Client Credentials** pane.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/8fba93316cfad4b33927681e9a77a43b4334b4855b4330a72aa4f86d159bdba9-parnter_portal_download_credentials.png" />

![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/03/Screenshot-2022-03-31-at-4.28.02-PM-1-1024x471.png)

4. Click **Download** to download the client ID, client secret, and UUID in a text file.

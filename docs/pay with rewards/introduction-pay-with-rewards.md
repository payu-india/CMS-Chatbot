Pay with Rewards enables payment through different loyalty & reward program currencies held by your customers. Using this payment option, your customers can pay whole amount or partial amount using reward points and the rest by any standard instrument like cards, UPI, Net Banking, etc. PayU has **partnered with TwidPay** (a reward point network) to enable Pay with Rewards.

The following flow diagram illustrates the flow with Pay with Rewards:

![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/05/Screenshot-2022-05-02-at-10.48.48-AM-1024x332.png)

PayU supports the following issuers, our program partner:

* Flipkart SuperCoin
* Timespoints
* Gaana
* Ola
* TWID Loyalty

PayU supports the following reward players:

1. TWID
2. Loyalty Rewards

This section includes the answers for frequently asked questions about integrating PayU with Interakt for WhatsApp Business, including payment links and in-chat payments.

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  **Reference**: For integration steps, refer to [Interakt for WhatsApp Business](doc:interakt-for-whatsapp-business) and [Integrate PayU with Interakt](doc:integrate-payu-with-interakt).
</Callout>


<Accordion title="1. What does PayU offer on Interakt for WhatsApp Business?" icon="fa-info-circle">
  PayU is partnered with Interakt so businesses using Interakt CRM can natively integrate PayU as their payment aggregator. For setup steps, refer to [Integrate PayU with Interakt](doc:integrate-payu-with-interakt).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Do I need a PayU merchant account before integrating with Interakt?" icon="fa-info-circle">
  Yes. Register for a merchant account on PayU before starting integration. For more information, refer to [Register for a Merchant Account](doc:register-for-a-merchant-account-on-dashboard).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="3. How do customers pay through WhatsApp with PayU and Interakt?" icon="fa-info-circle">
  Merchants can send payment links to customers through Interakt on WhatsApp. Customers complete payment through PayU's secure payment flow. For a walkthrough, refer to the video tutorial on [Interakt for WhatsApp Business](doc:interakt-for-whatsapp-business).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="4. Can I integrate directly with WhatsApp for Business without Interakt?" icon="fa-info-circle">
  PayU provides a suite of APIs to integrate directly with the WhatsApp for Business platform. For more information, refer to [CommercePro Checkout for WooCommerce](doc:commercepro-platform-for-woocommerce).
</Accordion>

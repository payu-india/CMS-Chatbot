To enable PayU payment gateway for Zoho Billing:

1. Log on to Zoho Billing.
2. Click  the **Settings** button on the toolbar.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/5b53bb1-zoho_billing_step2.png" />

   The *Settings* page is displayed.

3. Select the **Payment Gateway** option under **Online Payments**.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/98ee6e5-zoho_billing_step3.png" />

4. Navigate to **PayU** and then click **Set up Now**.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e59bc41-zoho_billing_step4.png" />

   The *Configure Gateway* pop-up page is displayed similar to the following screenshot:

<Image align="center" className="border" width="322px" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/020f501-Screenshot_2023-12-11_at_4.16.22_PM.png" />

5. Enter your merchant key in the **Key** field.

> **Note**: You need to register or create an account on [PayU Dashboard](https://onboarding.payu.in/app/account) to get the merchant key and Salt. For viewing or generating your merchant key and Salt, refer to [Generate Merchant Key and Salt](doc:generate-merchant-key-and-salt-on-payu-dashboard)

6. Enter your Salt in the **Salt** field.
7. Click **Save**.

To enable PayU payment gateway for Zoho Inventory:

1. Log on to Zoho Inventory.
2. Click  the **Settings** button on the toolbar.

   The _Settings_ page is displayed.

3. Select the **Customer Payments** option under **Online Payments**.

5. Enter your merchant key in the **Key** field.

> **Note**: You need to register or create an account on [PayU Dashboard](https://onboarding.payu.in/app/account) to get the merchant key and Salt. For viewing or generating your merchant key and Salt, refer to [Generate Merchant Key and Salt](doc:generate-merchant-key-and-salt-on-payu-dashboard)

6. Enter your Salt in the **Salt** field.
7. Click **Save**.

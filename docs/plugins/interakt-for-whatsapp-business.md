Interakt is a WhatsApp Business solution provider that helps fast-growing businesses and brands boost their online sales, and also enables them to automate their customer support. PayU is partnered with Interakt and if you are using Interakt CRM for your business, you can natively integrate with PayU as your payment aggregator.

> 👍 Before you begin:
> 
> Register for a account with PayU before you start integration. For more information, refer to [Register for a Merchant Account](doc:register-for-a-merchant-account-on-dashboard).

## What PayU offers?

PayU offers the following capabilities on WhatsApp platform:

- **Interakt for WhatsApp Business**: Refer to [Integrate PayU with Interakt](doc:integrate-payu-with-interakt)

> 📘 Reference:
> 
> PayU provides suite of APIs to directly integrate with WhatsApp for Business platform. For more information, refer to [CommercePro Checkout for WooCommerce](doc:commercepro-platform-for-woocommerce).

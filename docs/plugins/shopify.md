## What PayU Offers?

PayU offers the following capabilities on Shopify platform:

* **PayU Shopify Plugin**:  The PayU India plugin app on Shopify allows merchants to accept payments on their Shopify website. This also has an in-built functionality to allow merchants to initiate refunds from Shopify dashboard for all payments made via PayU PG. You can log on to Shopify and quickly set up PayU as a payment mode for your customers. When your customer completes shopping and does check out, the customer is taken to PayU to make a secure payment. After your customer makes a payment on the PayU Payments page, the order is confirmed, and the customer is redirected back to Shopify. For more information, refer to [Integration with Shopify](doc:integrate-with-shopify)
* **Offers on Shopify Page**: Refer to [Enable Offers on your Shopify Page](doc:enable-offers-on-your-shopify-page).
* **Reconcile PayU Transactions on Shopify**: Refer to [Reconcile Shopify Transactions](doc:reconcile-shopify-transactions)
* **Integrate Affordability Widget**: Refer to [Affordability Widget Integration for Shopify](doc:affordability-widget-integration-for-shopify)
* **CommercePro Checkout app for Shopify**: Refer to [Install CommercePro Checkout App](doc:install-commercepro-checkout-app)
* **Onsite Payment plugin**: Refer to [Enable Onsite Payments on Shopify](doc:enable-onsite-payments-on-shopify).
* **SKU-based Offers**: Refer to [Configure SKU-Based Offers for Shopify](doc:configure-sku-based-offers-shopify)

If you encounter issues while integration, refer to [Troubleshooting Magento Integration](doc:troubleshooting-magento-integration)

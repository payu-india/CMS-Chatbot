After you set up your account in Fynd, you can integrate PayU India as the payment platform for your customers.

The integration with Fynd involves the following steps:

1. [Configure PayU India PG](#configure-payU-india-pg)
2. [Activate PayU India PG](#activate-payU-india-pg)

## Activate PayU PG

1. Select** Settings** from the menu on the left pane, scroll-down and select **Payments** from the right pane.
2. Select **General Settings** .

   The PayU India PG tile s displayed with the status inactive.

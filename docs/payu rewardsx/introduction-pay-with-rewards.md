## What is Pay With Rewards?

Pay With Rewards is an innovative payment solution that allows customers to **seamlessly combine their loyalty points with regular payment methods** like UPI or cards in a single transaction. Built on PayU's cutting-edge split-pay engine, it aggregates 10+ leading reward platforms including Flipkart SuperCoins, Twid, Zillion, and Loylty Rewardz, giving you access to **1.2 billion+ loyalty users** across India's banking and retail ecosystems.

## How It Works – Split-Pay Magic

Our proprietary **split-pay engine** intelligently divides each transaction into two synchronized parts:

1. **Rewards Leg** – Instantly redeems available loyalty points
2. **Payment Leg** – Processes remaining amount via UPI/cards

If either part fails, the entire transaction automatically rolls back, ensuring a unified, risk-free checkout experience.

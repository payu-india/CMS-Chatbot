## UBO and Senior Management Declarations

The following details may need to be disclosed as a part of your merchant onboarding:

* UBO Declaration
* UBO Type
* Senior Management

Where the customer or the owner of the controlling interest is: 

* An entity listed on a stock exchange in India, or 
* iIt is an entity resident in jurisdictions notified by the Central Government and listed on stock exchanges in such jurisdictions, or
* It is a subsidiary of such listed entities; it is not necessary to identify and verify the identity of any shareholder or beneficial owner of such entities.

### UBO (Ultimate Beneficiary Owner) Declaration

Please declare the information regarding all the stakeholders based on their ownership: 

| Business Entity                                             | Shareholding % |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Companies (Public, Private) & LLP                           | > 10%          |
| Partnership                                                 | > 10%          |
| Trust                                                       | > /=10%        |
| Unincorporated associations/Body of Individuals (societies) | > 15%          |

### UBO(Ultimate Beneficiary Owner) Type

UBO Details may vary based on origin of the owner:

| UBO Type    | Details Required                                                   |
| :---------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Indian UBO  | UBO Name, PAN, Nationality - Indian, Ownership %, DOB and Address. |
| Foreign UBO | UBO Name, Nationality, Ownership %, Document of Address Proof      |

### Senior Management Details

Please declare the information regarding the senior stakeholders on within the organization based on your business entity:

| Category        | Details Required                                                                                     |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Partnership/LLP | 1. Please declare all applicable partners <br> 2. Please declare all KMPs (Key Management Personnel) |
| Public/Private  | 1. Please declare all Directors <br> 2. Please declare all KMPs (Key Management Personnel)           |

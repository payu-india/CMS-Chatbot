You can generate and download various reports to analyse the performance of your business and Payouts.  To generate the Payouts report:

1. Click the **Reports** menu of the navigation bar to visit **Reports** screen.![](https://files.readme.io/066f8da-Screenshot_2023-09-29_at_11.00.27_AM.png)2. Select **Payouts** tile. On clicking, the Generate Reports page gets displayed.![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-251-1024x536.png)3. Select **Payouts** from the **Select Report Type** drop-down list (if not selected). By default, the report type is selected as **Payouts**.

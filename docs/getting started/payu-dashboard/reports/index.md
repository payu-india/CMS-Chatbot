The report section enables you to generate, download and schedule the reports for the following dashboard variables:

* **Transaction**: These reports will fetch data for your transactions like payment, Customer Information, and Status.
* **Settlements**: These reports will show you settlements initiated, paused, and processed with details like service fees.
* **Refunds**: These reports will fetch you the details of refunds that were initiated and their statuses through the cycle.
* **TDRReport**: These are monthly invoices from PayU for service fees and tax.
* **Payouts**: These reports will show you the payouts data like amount disbursed, beneficiary and status.
* **Buttons**: These reports will fetch you the transactional data like amount collected and status for your payment buttons
* **PaymentsLinks**: These reports will fetch you the transactional data like the amount collected and status for your payment links.
* **Invoices**: These reports will fetch you the transactional data like the amount collected and the status of your invoices.

The following sections describes how to generate various reports:

* [Generate Reports](https://docs.payu.in/docs/generate-reports)
* [Schedule Reports](https://docs.payu.in/docs/schedule-reports)
* [Generate Payouts Reports](https://docs.payu.in/docs/generate-payouts-reports)

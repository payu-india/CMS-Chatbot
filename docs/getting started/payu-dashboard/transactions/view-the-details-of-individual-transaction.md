To view the specific settlement details, including the settlement breakup and net settle amount along with the transactions in the specific settlement:

1. Enter the **UTR Number** in the search column and click **Search** to view the results
2.  Click UTR number to view the complete settlement details including the fields **Settlement amount, Settled as on,** and **Total transactions**.

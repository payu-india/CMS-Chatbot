## Update Website Details

On the **Website** tab of the page, you can view and edit the **Website or Application URLs** and **Product or Service** details.

To update your website details:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Edit Profile > Website** tab.

   The **Website** tab is displayed on the *Profile* page.

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard onboarding profile - The Website tab is displayed on the Profile page." className="border" border={true} src="https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-116.png" />

2. Click **Update** then a new pop-up window with website details appears.

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard - Click Update then a new pop-up window with website details appears" className="border" border={true} src="https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-117.png" />

3. Enter the required details in the respective fields and click **Next**.

![PayU Dashboard - Enter the required details in the respective fields and click Next](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-118.png)

4. Enter the **OTP** received on the verified email ID or mobile number.
5. Click **Update Website Details** to complete the action.
6. Expand the **Product/Services** Details section.
7. Update the URL of the page containing the product or service details in the respective field.
8. Select the check box against the features present on your website.
9. Click **Submit** to complete the action.

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard - Click Submit to complete the action" className="border" border={true} src="https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-119.png" />

### Update Documents

The **Documents** tab enables you to view and upload the proofs, **Service agreement** and produce the **E-SIGN**.

You can upload your proof using the **Upload** button and view the accepted proofs in the respective section.

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard - Update Documents" className="border" border={true} src="https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-120.png" />

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard - image 121" className="border" border={true} src="https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-121.png" />

After the documents are verified, the service agreement is displayed. You can **Accept** the service agreement and E-Sign it.

To export and download the settlement records:

1. Click the **Download** menu to view the options.

   The menu options under the **Download** menu are displayed.

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard - The menu options under the Download menu are displayed." src="https://files.readme.io/8c560cb-Screenshot_2023-09-22_at_8.09.37_PM.png" />

2. Choose **Select Fields** from the **Download** menu.

   The *Column Settings* pop-up page is displayed.

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard - The Column Settings pop-up page is displayed." src="https://files.readme.io/663e521-Screenshot_2023-09-22_at_8.11.17_PM.png" />

3. Select the desired check boxes from the **Column Settings** pop-up window.
4. Click **Save** to complete the action.
5. Select any of the following format to generate the report:

   * **csv**
   * **xslx**

    A pop-up window will display the status of the generated report.

<Image align="center" alt="PayU Dashboard reports - Select any of the following format to generate the report:" src="https://files.readme.io/3201051-Screenshot_2023-09-22_at_7.51.46_PM.png" />

6. Perform any of the following steps:

   * Click **Download report** option to complete the action.
   * Click **Share** and enter the recipient email ID.

    The Share Report pop-up window is displayed where you need to enter the e-mail ID.

   ![PayU Dashboard reports - The Share Report pop-up window is displayed where you need to enter the e-mail ID.](https://files.readme.io/64f5077-Screenshot_2023-09-22_at_8.14.19_PM.png)
7. Click **Share** to complete the action.

> 📘 Note:
>
> You can share the report to multiple email IDs by entering the comma-separated entries.

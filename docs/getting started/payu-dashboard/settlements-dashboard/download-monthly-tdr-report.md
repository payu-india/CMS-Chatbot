You can download the monthly invoice using the **Download Monthly TDR report** option given on the top right corner of the settlement overview page.

To download monthly TDR report:

1. Click the **Download Monthly TDR Report** tab.

   The *Download Invoice* pop-up page is displayed.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/3201051-Screenshot_2023-09-22_at_7.51.46_PM.png" />

2. Select the month and year from the **Select year** and **Select month** drop-down lists for which the invoice is required.
3. Select invoice type that you wish to download from the drop-down list.
4. Click **Download** to complete the action.

## View the details of individual settlement

To view the specific settlement details, including the settlement breakup and net settle amount along with the transactions in the specific settlement:

1. Enter the **UTR Number** in the search column and click **Search** to view the results
2.  Click UTR number to view the complete settlement details including the fields **Settlement amount, Settled as on,** and **Total transactions**.

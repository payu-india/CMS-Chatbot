This module provides the following functions:

* **Collated View Of Sales**: Sales, orders, and returns analytics for predicting mismatches and net profit.
* **Collated View Of Payments**: Receivables, due settlements, and commission deduction analytics for better predicting mismatches and accounting.
* **Convenient Accounting**: Two-way reconciliation, that is, settlements done in bank and seller account with an understanding of expected vs. actual.
* **Notifications and Alerts**: Relevant notifications and alerts for smooth business operations.
* **Analytics For better Operations**: Overview for individual elements and a bird-eye view of complete seller account operations.

## Link Amazon Seller Account on Dashboard

You can link your Amazon seller account on the PayU Dashboard to get a collated view of payment and sales.

To link your Amazon seller account:

1. Navigate to **Dashboard > Sales & Earnings > Overview**.

   The Sales & Earnings Overview page is displayed.

![PayU Dashboard - The Sales & Earnings Overview page is displayed.](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/09/Dashboard_Sales_Earnings-1024x737.png)

2. Click **Link Account** on the **Amazon Marketplace** pane.

   A pop-up page displayed similar to the following screenshot:

![PayU Dashboard - A pop-up page displayed similar to the following screenshot:](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/09/Dashboard_Amazon_seller_account_add-1024x699.png)

3. Enter your merchant ID for Amazon in the **Merchant Id** field.
4. Enter your MWS token provided by Amazon in the **MWS Token** field.
5. Click **Grant Access**.

## Link Flipkart Seller Account on Dashboard

You can link your Flipkart seller account on the PayU Dashboard to get a collated view of payment and sales.

To link your Flipkart seller account:

1. Navigate to **Dashboard > Sales & Earnings > Overview**.

   The Sales & Earnings Overview page is displayed.

![PayU Dashboard - The Sales & Earnings Overview page is displayed.](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/09/Dashboard_Sales_Earnings-1024x737.png)

2. Click **Link Account** on the **Flipkart Marketplace** pane.

   A pop-up page displayed similar to the following screenshot:

![PayU Dashboard - A pop-up page displayed similar to the following screenshot:](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/09/Dashboard_Flipkar_acct_link-893x1024.png)

3. Enter your App ID for Flipkart in the **App Id** field.
4. Enter your App secret key provided by Flipkart in the **App Secret key** field.
5. Enter the location identifier in the **Location Id** field.
6. Enter your Flipkart merchant account user name in the **Username** field.
7. Enter your Flipkart merchant account password in the **Password** field.
8. Click **Grant Access**.

## **Categorize and View Beneficiaries**

To categorize and view the beneficiaries on the page:

1. Click the **Filter** drop-down menu at the top-right corner of the page.
2. Select the required check boxes against each category from the menu.
3. Click **Apply** to get the results.

**Note**: Use the **Reset** to clear the selection if required.

![PayU Dashboard - Click Apply to get the results](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-68.png)

**Note:** To view only the active beneficiaries, select the **Active** tab. Similarly, only inactive beneficiaries are displayed on the **Inactive** ta&#x62;*.*

The action field enables you to perform the following functions:

* Edit the **Beneficiary** details
* **Activate or deactivate** the beneficiary
* Make a payment to the beneficiary

## Money Transfer

To transfer money to a beneficiary:

1. Add the money to your **DMT account**.
2. Post sender and beneficiary details through API.
3. Get commission and GST Settlement.

## Update a Beneficiary Details

To update a particular beneficiary detail:

1. Select the **Actions** menu and click ellipsis to choose **Edit**.

The **Update Beneficiary** page is displayed.

![PayU Dashboard - The Update Beneficiary page is displayed.](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-70.png)

1. Enter the new details in the required fields.
2. Click **Save** to complete the action.

## Export Beneficiary Details

To export the beneficiary details:

1. Click **Download** to select the desired format (CSV or XLSX) from the drop-down list.

A dialog box displays the status of the report.

1. Click **Download Report** to complete the action.

![PayU Dashboard reports - Click Download Report to complete the action](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-67.png)

## UBO and Senior Management Declarations

The following details may need to be disclosed as a part of your merchant onboarding:

* UBO
* Senior Management

Additionally, please note that following details will be required as a part of your onboarding:

### UBO Declaration

Please declare the information regarding all the stakeholders based on their ownership:

| Business Entity                                             | Shareholding % |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Companies (Public, Private) & LLP                           | 10%            |
| Partnership                                                 | 10%            |
| Trust                                                       | 10%            |
| Unincorporated associations/Body of Individuals (societies) | 15%            |

### Senior Management Details

Please declare the information regarding the senior stakeholders on within the organization based on your business entity:

| Category        | Details Required                                                                                     |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Partnership/LLP | 1. Please declare all applicable partners <br/> 2. Please declare all KMPs (Key Management Personnel) |
| Public/Private  | 1. Please declare all Directors <br/> 2. Please declare all KMPs (Key Management Personnel)           |

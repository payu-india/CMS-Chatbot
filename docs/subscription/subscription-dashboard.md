The Subscription Dashboard is used to create subscription payment links, upload the subscription registration or recurring transactions in bulk. The following sections describe the procedure to perform these tasks on Subscription Dashboard:

- [Create a Subscription Payment Link](doc:create-a-subscription-payment-link-using-dashboard)
- [Upload Registration Transactions in Bulk](doc:upload-registration-transactions-in-bulk)
- [Upload Recurring Transactions in Bulk](doc:upload-recurring-transactions-in-bulk)

After you create a subscription payment link or complete bulk upload transactions, you can manage them as described in the following:

- [Manage Subscription Payment Links](doc:manage-subscription-payment-links) 
- [Manage Bulk Upload Transactions](doc:manage-subscription-bulk-upload-transactions). 

Also, you access subscription mandates as described in [Access Subscription Mandates](doc:access-subscription-mandates).

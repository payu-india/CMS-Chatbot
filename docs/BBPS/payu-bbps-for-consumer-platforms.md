## What is PayU BBPS for Consumer Platforms?

Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) — also known as Bharat Connect — is an RBI-mandated, NPCI-operated national infrastructure for recurring bill payments. As a Consumer Platform (also called an **Agent Institution** or **COU — Consumer Operating Unit**), you give your users the ability to discover and pay bills for any registered BBPS biller in India — directly from within your own app or platform.

PayU holds dual BBPS licences — **Biller Operating Unit (BOU)** and **Consumer Operating Unit (COU)**. As your COU partner, PayU connects your platform to the BBPS network, giving your users instant access to **22,000+ live billers** across **20+ categories** — electricity, loans, insurance, school fees, DTH, government taxes, and more — through a single API integration or a ready-made white-label solution.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="default">
  ### **Why BBPS for Your Platform?**

  Previously, consumer platforms had to integrate individually with hundreds of billers — a massive engineering and commercial effort. With BBPS via PayU, one integration gives your platform access to the entire national biller network instantly.
</Callout>

## How It Works

Once integrated with PayU as your COU partner, here is how a bill payment flows through your platform.

### Standard Fetch-and-Pay Flow (e.g., Electricity, Loan EMI, School Fees)

1. **Step 1: User Selects Category & Biller** — Your user opens the bill payments section, selects a category (e.g., Electricity) and then a biller (e.g., BESCOM). The biller list is fetched in real time via PayU's Get Billers API.
2. **Step 2: User Enters Unique Identifier** — The user enters their unique identifier — consumer number, loan account ID, student ID, or policy number — depending on the biller category.
3. **Step 3: Your Platform Calls PayU Fetch API** — Your platform sends a Fetch request to PayU → PayU (as COU) routes it to NPCI (BBPCU) → NPCI routes to the relevant BOU → BOU calls the biller system.
4. **Step 4: Bill Details Returned to Your Platform** — The biller returns bill details (amount due, due date, additional fields). These travel back through the chain and PayU returns them to your platform via API response.
5. **Step 5: User Reviews & Pays** — Your app displays the fetched bill details. The user selects a payment mode and completes payment. Your platform sends a Payment Posting request to PayU.
6. **Step 6: Payment Confirmed** — PayU routes the payment posting through COU → NPCI → BOU → Biller. Confirmation flows back to your platform. You display the receipt to the user.
7. **Step 7: Funds Settled on T+1** — On T+1, NPCI debits your (Agent Institution's) sponsor bank account. PayU manages all downstream biller settlements — you receive a consolidated settlement statement.

### BBPS Click Pay Flow

1. Biller generates a Click Pay link and sends it to your user via WhatsApp or SMS.
2. User taps the link → device shows all BBPS-registered apps that can process Click Pay.
3. User selects your app → opens directly on the payment screen with all bill details pre-filled (no category selection, biller search, or identifier entry needed).
4. User confirms and pays → transaction proceeds like a standard BBPS payment posting.

### UPMS (Autopay Mandate) Flow

1. User registers a recurring bill (e.g., electricity) via the UPMS registration API — one-time setup.
2. When the biller generates a new bill instance, PayU pushes bill details to your platform via callback API.
3. If autopay is enabled, the payment is processed automatically using the user's saved instrument via PayU PG — no user action required.
4. User receives a payment notification. Duplicate payment alerts are sent if the bill has already been paid from another registered app, preventing accidental double payment.

## What You Will Need (Prerequisites)

Before going live as a BBPS Consumer Platform via PayU, ensure the following:

- **A PayU Account / Commercial Agreement:** Complete onboarding with PayU to obtain your `client_id` and `client_secret` for OAuth 2.0 API authentication.
- **Agent Institution (AI) Registration with NPCI:** Your platform must be registered as an Agent Institution (AI) with NPCI. PayU, as your COU partner, facilitates this registration process.
- **NPCI-Compliant Front-End Screens — API-Based Integration Only:** If choosing the API-based path, your front-end UI screens must be built per NPCI's UI guidelines. NPCI review and screen approval is required before go-live.
- **Technical Team for API Integration:** A team capable of integrating RESTful APIs: Get Categories, Get Billers by Category, Fetch, Payment Posting, Status Check, Complaint APIs, and — for prepaid recharges — MNP and Get Plans APIs.
- **SSO or Redirect Capability — TSP White-Label Path Only:** If using PayU's white-label TSP solution, you need an SSO-based redirect from your app to PayU's white-label screens (web, mobile web, or native SDK).
- **Android & iOS App Registration with NPCI — Click Pay Only:** To process BBPS Click Pay links, your apps must be registered with NPCI. You also need to build deep-link / intent-handling capability to open the pre-filled payment screen.
- **Callback API — UPMS Only:** To receive push notifications for new bill instances from billers, you must expose and integrate a callback API endpoint.
- **UAT Completion & NPCI Sign-Off:** Full end-to-end testing of all bill payment flows must be completed in the UAT environment and signed off by NPCI before production go-live.

## What Happens After a Payment

Once a user on your platform completes a bill payment, here is what happens across all parties:

1. **Real-Time Confirmation to Your Platform:** A payment confirmation response is returned to your platform via API immediately. Display the receipt to your user — including transaction ID, amount, biller name, and timestamp.
2. **Biller System Updated in Real Time:** The payment confirmation is routed by PayU (via NPCI → BOU) to the biller's system. The biller's records are updated and a BBPS payment receipt is generated.
3. **NPCI Clears Funds on T+1:** On T+1, NPCI debits your platform's (Agent Institution's) sponsor bank account for the total value of all bill payments processed on T Day via your Agent Institution ID.
4. **PayU Manages All Downstream Settlement:** PayU handles settlements to every individual biller via its BOU licence. You deal with one counterparty only — PayU — for all settlements.
5. **Complaints Handled via NPCI Mechanism:** User complaints (e.g., bill paid but not updated, wrong amount debited) are raised via the BBPS Complaints API. NPCI's standardised grievance mechanism handles resolution with the biller. Your platform is the user-facing interface for complaint submission and status tracking.
6. **Duplicate Payment Prevention via UPMS:** If a registered bill has already been paid from another UPMS-linked app, your platform receives a push notification — preventing accidental duplicate payment and improving user trust.
7. **Reporting & Reconciliation:** All transaction data is available via your PayU dashboard and reporting APIs in real time. Automated settlement statements are provided for end-of-day reconciliation.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  ### **Important — Server-Side Verification**

  Always confirm payment status using the PayU **Status Check API** on your server side before updating user records or displaying a final success screen. Do not rely solely on the payment posting API response.
</Callout>

## Ready to Integrate?

Choose the integration path that best fits your platform:

### Path 1 — API-Based Integration (Full Control, Custom UI)

_Best for: Platforms with dedicated tech teams that want complete UI control._

1. Obtain your `client_id` and `client_secret` from PayU.
2. Call `POST https://uat-accounts.payu.in/oauth/token` with `grant_type=client_credentials` to get your OAuth 2.0 access token. Use it for all subsequent API calls.
3. Integrate the full API suite in sequence: **Get Categories → Get Billers by Category → Fetch Bill → Payment Posting → Status Check → Complaint Registration & Status Check APIs.**
4. For prepaid recharges, also integrate: **MNP API** (circle/operator lookup) and **Get Plans API**.
5. For Click Pay: register your Android and iOS apps with NPCI; build deep-link handling to open the pre-filled payment screen directly.
6. For UPMS: integrate UPMS add/modify/delete mandate APIs and the callback API to receive bill push notifications from billers.
7. Build front-end screens per NPCI UI guidelines and submit for NPCI screen approval.
8. Complete full end-to-end UAT across all flows and receive NPCI sign-off.
9. Go live — your users can now pay any BBPS bill from within your platform.

### Path 2 — TSP White-Label Solution (Fastest Time-to-Market)

_Best for: Banks and platforms that want a ready-made, branded bill payment experience._

1. Implement SSO-based redirect from your app or portal to PayU's white-label bill payment screens — choose web redirect, mobile web redirect, or native SDK.
2. Customise the look and feel — colours, logo, fonts — to match your brand precisely. PayU's screens are already NPCI-compliant; no separate screen approval required.
3. All modules are included out of the box: User Management, Bill Payments, Complaints, Alerts & Reminders, Transaction History.
4. Complete integration testing and go live — your users experience a seamless, fully branded bill payment flow without leaving your ecosystem.

### Path 3 — Bank-Specific Products

_Best for: Banks wanting to offer BBPS to their customers or onboard their billers._

- **Bank Applications:** Enable bill payments for all BBPS billers on your net banking / mobile banking app using your own COU pipe.
- **Partner Applications:** Allow your partner apps to enable BBPS bill payments using your bank's COU pipe.
- **Bank Billers:** Onboard your bank's own billers (insurance, loans, etc.) onto BBPS using your bank's BOU pipe.
- **Partner Billers:** Onboard your bank's partner billers onto BBPS using your BOU pipe.

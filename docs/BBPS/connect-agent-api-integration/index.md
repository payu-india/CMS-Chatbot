The Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) provides an online platform for centralized bill payments.It is an integrated bill payment system that offers interoperable and accessible bill payment service to customers through a network of agents of registered members, enabling multiple payment modes and providing instant confirmation of payment.

National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) functions as the authorised Bharat Bill Payment Central Unit (BBPCU. NPCI is primarily engaged in enabling fast, efficient and secure real-time payments between banks and between banks and other entities.

## Features

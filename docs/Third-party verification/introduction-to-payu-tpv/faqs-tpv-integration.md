• **Is there any compliance requirement for TPV?**

    Yes, TPV is a mandatory requirement for businesses in the BFSI sector, particularly for those involved in Broking, Mutual Funds, and Securities.

• **How many pre-registered accounts are allowed?**\
      Merchants are allowed to pass details for up to four pre-registered bank accounts.

• **Which payment modes are supported?**\
      The supported payment modes include UPI and NetBanking.

• **Which platforms are supported?**\
        TPV integration supports the Desktop, M-web, SDK, and ICP platforms.

• **Which banks are supported for Net banking and UPI with TPV integration?**\
          Currently, PayU supports 29 banks for NetBanking. For UPI, support is available on all apps. For more information, refer to [Bank Codes for TPV](https://docs.payu.in/docs/bank-codes-for-tpv-copy).

• **Is TPV supported on UPI Intent?**\
         Yes, TPV is supported on UPI Intent.

• **Is TPV supported on UPI Collect?**\
          Yes, TPV is supported on UPI Collect.

• **Is Tez TPV supported for TPV?**\
          No, Tez TPV is not supported for TPV.

• **Is an upfront QR supported for TPV?**\
          No, upfront QR is not supported for TPV.

• **Will the customer's account information be visible on the PayU Checkout page?**\
    No, only the last four digits of the registered account will be visible, and other bank account details will be masked.

• **Can I pass more than four bank accounts with PayU Hosted or non-seamless integration?**\
    You can pass the details for up to four bank accounts. If more than four bank accounts are passed, the transaction will be rejected.

• **Can I pass the details of a beneficiary having two accounts with the same bank?**\
    Yes, merchants can pass details for beneficiaries having two or more accounts with the same bank.

• **Can I opt for TPV only on UPI?**\
    Yes, merchants can choose TPV only on UPI. In such cases, customers will see UPI options directly. Contact your PayU Key Account Manager (KAM) or contact [PayU Support](https://help.payu.in/).

• **Can I opt for TPV only on NetBanking?**\
    Yes, you can opt for TPV only on NetBanking. Customers will see NetBanking options directly. Contact your PayU Key Account Manager (KAM) or contact [PayU Support](https://help.payu.in/).

• **Is the convenience fee supported on UPI?**\
    No, the convenience fee is not supported on UPI.

• **Is the convenience fee supported on NetBanking?**\
    Yes, the convenience fee is supported on NetBanking.

• **Can we run offers on TPV?**\
    No, running offers on TPV is not supported.

• **Is a retry option available on TPV?**\
    No, a retry option is not available on TPV.

• **Are saved card payment options supported on TPV?**\
    No, saved card payment options are not supported on TPV.

• **Are preferred payment options supported on TPV?**\
    No, preferred payment options are not supported on TPV.

• **What happens if invalid bank details are passed to PayU?**\
     In such a scenario, a normal checkout process will be initiated.

NEFT (National Electronic Fund Transfer) or RTGS (eal-Time Gross Settlement) facilitates secure fund transfers from one bank account to another on a one-to-one basis. PayU EFTNET offers NEFT/RTGS as an payment mode through non-seamless (PayU Hosted) or seamless integration (Merchant Hosted or S2S). This part of the document provides the steps to integrate the EFTNET (NEFT/RTGS) and its other offerings:

* [PayU Hosted Checkout Integration - EFTNET](doc:payu-hosted-checkout-eftnet)
* [Merchant Hosted Checkout Integration - EFTNET](doc:collect-payments-with-eftnet-neftrtgs-seamless)
* [Reusable VANs Integration - EFTNET](doc:reusable-van-integration-neft)

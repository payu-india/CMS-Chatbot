title: LazyPay Pay in 3 — Overview 
---

LazyPay Pay-in-3, the product that lets your customers split the cost of their purchase into 3 simple, equal, interest\-free installments\. Customers pay the first installment upfront at the time of checkout, and the remaining two installments are automatically charged in the 2nd and 3rd month — with zero interest and zero hidden fees\.

Pay-in-3 is designed for everyday, mid\-ticket purchases, making it easier for customers to say yes to a purchase without straining their monthly budget, while merchants benefit from higher conversion and order value, with no added risk\.

Pay-in-3 is offered by LazyPay and integrates directly into your existing checkout flow\. Customers select Pay in 3 as a payment option at checkout, complete a quick one\-time verification, and get instant approval — no lengthy forms, no credit card required\.  

Before you begin:  
Register for an account with PayU before you start integration\. For more information, refer to [Register for a Merchant Account](https://docs.payu.in/docs/register-for-a-merchant-account-on-dashboard)\. 

## How Pay in 3 Works

1. __Customer selects Pay-in-3 at checkout__ — The option appears alongside other payment methods on your checkout page, along with an instalment breakdown so customers know exactly what they'll pay and when\.
2. __Instant eligibility check__ — The customer completes a quick mobile\-number based verification\.  A real\-time eligibility check is done in the background — no paperwork, no waiting\.
3. __First instalment charged immediately__ — One\-third of the order value is charged at the time of transaction, just like a regular payment\.
4. __Order is processed right away__ — There's no delay to the merchant\. Once the first instalment is successful, the order moves to fulfillment exactly as it would with any other payment method\.
5. __2nd and 3rd instalments auto\-collected__ — The remaining two instalments are automatically deducted in month 2 and month 3, on the same date each month\. The customer doesn't need to take any further action\.

 

## Pay-in-3 Benefits

Pay-in-3 offers the following benefits to both customers and merchants:

- __Simple, predictable payments__: Just 3 equal installments — no complex tenure choices, no interest calculators, no confusion\.
- __Zero interest, zero hidden charges__: Customers pay exactly one\-third of the order value each month\. What they see at checkout is what they pay\.
- __Instant approval__: Customers get a real\-time decision and can complete checkout in seconds\.
- __No documentation required__: No credit card, no income proof, no paperwork — just a quick mobile verification\.
- __Higher conversion and AOV for merchants__: By breaking the purchase price into smaller, manageable amounts, merchants can convert price\-sensitive customers and unlock higher average order values\.
- __Reduced cart abandonment__: Customers are more likely to complete checkout when the full payment isn't due upfront\.
- __Merchant gets paid upfront__: Merchants receive the full order value at the time of transaction — as Lazypay takes on the responsibility \(and risk\) of collecting the remaining installments from the customer\. There is no impact to your settlement cycle\.

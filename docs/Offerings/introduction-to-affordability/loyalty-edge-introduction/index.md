Customer loyalty is a customer's ongoing commitment to repeatedly purchase from or engage with a specific company over time, driven by satisfaction, positive experiences, and emotional connection with the brand. It represents both a mindset and a behavioral pattern where customers consistently choose one company over competitors.

## Why Loyalty?

You have to implement loyalty for your business:

* Increase Retention Rates
* Lower Acquisition Costs
* Revenue Growth

## PayU  Loyalty Rewards

The PayU Loyalty Edge integrates loyalty points into transaction processes, enabling your customers to use loyalty points as a payment method. It includes functionality for earning, redeeming, adjusting points, and handling refunds and chargebacks related to loyalty transactions. Key components include checkout integration, flexible payment methods, offers and loyalty benefits, refunds and adjustments, detailed reporting, and onboarding processes for merchants.

## Business Benefits for Merchants

* **Enhanced Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)**: Encourage repeat purchases and build long-term loyalty
* **Increased Average Order Value (AOV)**: Drive higher spending through strategic rewards
* **Reduced Customer Churn**: Create compelling incentives for customers to return
* **Higher Purchase Frequency**: Motivate customers to shop more often with milestone rewards
* **Personalized Experiences**: Deliver tailored rewards based on customer preferences
* **Emotional Connection**: Foster deeper brand attachment through recognition and rewards

## How to launch a loyalty campaign

To launch a loyalty campaign using PayU Dashboard:

1. [Enable Loyalty Edge](doc:enable-loyalty-edge)
2. [Set up a Loyalty](https://docs.payu.in/docs/launch-loyalty-program-using-dashboard/#step-1-setup-loyalty)

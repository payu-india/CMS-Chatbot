Virtual Cards are digital-only payment instruments (typically Visa, Mastercard, or RuPay) that function like physical debit or credit cards but exist only within a mobile app or web interface. According to PayU's documentation, they are primarily used to enable secure online transactions for digital wallets or Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPI).

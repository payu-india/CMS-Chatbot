## Purpose

Introduce **Zion** — PayU's subscription automation platform for merchants who want billing plans and invoice execution without building full orchestration.

## When to use this

* Complex or variable billing plans per customer
* Insurance, EdTech, or multi-plan subscriptions
* Minimize backend billing scheduler work

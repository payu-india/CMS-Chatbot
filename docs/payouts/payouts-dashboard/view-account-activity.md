With Payouts Dashboard, you can view your account activity while checking balance and payouts volume.

Before adding money to your account, you can:

* Track account analytics
* Check your account balance
* Check Payout volume

To check the account balance:

1. Click the virtual account drop-down list similar to the following figure:

![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-207.png)

2. Select the required bank account from the list, whose balance you want to check.

![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-208.png)

3. Select an appropriate duration to view the account activity, using corresponding Date filter options as shown in figure below and click **Apply** to save changes.

![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-210.png)

The account balance gets displayed under **Available Balance**.

> **Note**: You can also check **Payouts Volume** for the selected account similar to the following figure:

![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/image-213.png)

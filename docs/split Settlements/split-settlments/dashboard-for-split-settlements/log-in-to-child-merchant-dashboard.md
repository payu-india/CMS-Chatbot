The child merchants (sub-accounts) will use the same Dashboard to check their transactions, settlements, and reports. The child merchants must use the same steps as described in the [Log in to Dashboard](doc:log-in-to-dashboard) section to log in to Dashboard.

> 📘 Note:
>
> If the onboarding is not complete for the child merchants (after entering their name, mobile number, and e-mail address is entered), an e-mail is sent to their e-mail address similar to the following screenshot:

![](https://devguide.payu.in/wordpress/index.php/wp-json/getobject?keyname=uploads/2022/07/child_merchant_incomplete_mail-1024x929.png)

Click **Verify PAN details now** on the mail to complete the details. For more information on completing the details, refer to [Adding Sub-Accounts](doc:adding-sub-accounts).

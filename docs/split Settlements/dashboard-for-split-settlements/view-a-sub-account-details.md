To update a sub-account or child merchant details on Split Settlements Dashboard:

1. Navigate to the **Manage Sub-accounts** page.
2. Click the account ID number under the **Account ID** column against the sub-account entry to view the details.

   The sub-account details are displayed on the top panel of the page, similar to the following screenshot.

   [block:image]{"images":[{"image":["https://files.readme.io/655242c13439115e5096c8614f60bb8ee027054a240454abbdb2c322123b2897-Screenshot_2025-02-17_at_11.14.12_AM.png",null,null],"align":"center"}]}[/block]

If the bank details are incomplete, complete the details. For more information, refer to [Add a Sub-Account](doc:add-a-sub-account).

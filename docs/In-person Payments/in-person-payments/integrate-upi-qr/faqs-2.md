## General

* What is the PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API?\
  The PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API is a set of APIs that allows you to accept UPI payments from customers through a UPI QR code, both in-store and online. The API provides features such as generating QR codes, scanning QR codes, and processing payments.
* What types of UPI QR codes can I generate with the API?\
  OR
* I have been using PayU for payment integration for the last few years with our application. I am looking for QR Code integration to accept UPI payments for our application.

Yes, PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API supports the generation of dynamic UPI QR codes and static UPI QR codes. Dynamic UPI QR codes allow for dynamic transaction amounts while static UPI QR codes allow any amount which the customer chooses to pay.

PayU offers Omnichannel integration, and choose the integration that suits your business needs and refer to the corresponding integration documentation:

UPI QR\
POS Cloud Integration\
POS Mobile SDK Integration\
Integrated Dynamic Storefront QR\
What types of UPI payments are supported by the API?\
The PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API supports UPI payments through the BHIM UPI app and other UPI-enabled mobile banking apps.

What are the fees or TDR for using the PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API?\
The fees for using the PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API depend on the type and volume of transactions you process. You can contact your PayU Key Account Manager.

If I use the Bharat QR or BQR Integration, is there any TDR?\
You need to contact your PayU Key Account Manager to know the details of the charges for using the BQR integration and QR Code payment.

How does the PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API ensure the security of my payments?\
The Omnichannel UPI QR APIs use secure encryption and tokenization technologies to ensure the security of your payments. Additionally, all transactions are processed according to UPI guidelines and regulations.

Is there a limit to the number of UPI QR codes I can generate with the API?\
No, there is no limit to the number of UPI QR codes you can generate with the PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API. You can generate as many QR codes as you need to support your payment processing needs.

If I have questions or issues with the Omnichannel UPI QR API, is there any support available?\
Yes, PayU provides technical support to help you with any questions or issues you may have with the PayU Omnichannel UPI QR API. You can contact the PayU support team or raise a ticket.

How do I enable QR as a payment mode for my PayU Hosted Checkout integration?\
You need to contact your PayU Key Account Manager (KAM) to enable QR as a payment mode.

Which API do I need to use for Dynamic Bharat QR on UPI (DBQR) on UPI?\
You need to use the Dynamic Bharat QR Generation API for Dynamic Bharat QR on UPI.

What is the purpose of Insta Static Bharat QR APIs?\
The Insta Static Bharat QR APIs allow you to integrate to accept payments through static Bharat QR codes. This API is designed to simplify the payment process for you and your customers by enabling fast and secure payments through a QR code.

What is the difference between the Static QR and Dynamic QR? .\
Static QR codes can be posted on a standee, and the customers can scan these QR codes and pay any amount they choose, whereas Dynamic QR codes are embedded with the amount (which the merchant wants to collect) and either need a dynamic QR code or is printed over an invoice.  PayU offers the following:

Insta Static Bharat QR and Integrated Static Bharat QR APIs are used for generating Static QR codes, where the payment details are pre-configured by the merchant.\
Dynamic QR for Merchant Hosted APIs are used for generating Dynamic QR with Merchant Hosted checkout, where the payment details can be updated in real-time based on the transaction.\
What are the benefits of using the Insta Static Bharat QR API?\
The benefits of using the Insta Static Bharat QR API include faster payment processing, reduced errors and discrepancies, and improved convenience and accessibility for customers. This feature also enables merchants to accept payments from a wide range of payment methods, including UPI, credit and debit cards, and net banking.

What types of merchants can use the Insta Static Bharat QR API?\
The Insta Static Bharat QR API is designed for a variety of merchants, including small and medium businesses, online and offline stores, and government entities. This API is particularly useful for merchants who want to offer customers a convenient and secure payment method.

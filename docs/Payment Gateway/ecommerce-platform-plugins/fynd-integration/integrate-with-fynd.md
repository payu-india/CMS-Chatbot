After you set up your account in Fynd, you can integrate PayU India as the payment platform for your customers.

The integration with Fynd involves the following steps:

1. [Configure PayU India PG](#configure-payU-india-pg)
2. [Activate PayU India PG](#activate-payU-india-pg)

## Configure PayU India PG

To configure the PayU India PG on your Fynd store:

1. Log on to your Fynd Dashboard.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/d23f9c3-fynd_home.png" />

2. Expand your store and select **Settings** from the menu on the left pane.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/272f289-fynd_settings_screen.png" />

3. Scroll-down and select **Payments** under **Cart & Payments**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/f29cd79-fynd_settings_cart_payments.png" />

4. Select **General Settings**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/37dba59-fynd_payments_page.png" />

5. Click **Add Payment Gateway**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/25003ca-fynd_add_pymt_gateway_button.png" />

6. Search and select **PayU India**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/7607a33-fynd_payu_india_search_select.png" />

7. Click **Install** at the top-right corner.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/4cf65ad-fynd_payu_india_plugin_page.png" />

8. Click **Accept and Continue** to provide 

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/9ec37d6-fynd_payu_provide_permission.png" />

   The PayU India Dashboard login is displayed.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/79bb0de-fynd_payu_login_page.png" />

9. Enter your PayU India credentials and click **Login**.
10. Click **Allow access to this account**.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/9842c1d-fynd_allow_access.png" />

The "All Set" message is displayed similar to the following screenshot:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/5cc071e-fynd_setup_complete.png" />

10. Follow the steps as described in [Activate PayU PG](#activate-payu-pg) to activate PayU PG on your store.

## Activate PayU PG

1. Select **Settings** from the menu on the left pane, scroll-down and select **Payments** from the right pane.
2. Select **General Settings** .

   The PayU India PG tile s displayed with the status inactive.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/67ee083-fynd_payu_india_pg_inactive.png" />

3. Select the **PayU India** tile.
4. Click the **Inactive** toggle button to make it active similar to the following screenshot:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/82d9a4f-fynd_payu_activate.png" />

5. Click **Save**.

  The PayU India PG tile is displayed with the status active.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/fbae9cb-fynd_payu_pg_active_status.png" />

After the above steps are completed, **PayU India** will be available as a payment option for buyers on your website's checkout page.

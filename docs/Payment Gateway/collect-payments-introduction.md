A **payment gateway** enables businesses to securely accept payments from customers through their website or mobile application. The **PayU Payment Gateway** acts as a secure bridge between your customer, your application, and the banking networks to process transactions safely and reliably.

## Advantages

<Accordion title="Secure and Reliable Transactions" icon="fa-shield-halved">
  * Ensures **safe, encrypted payment processing**
  * Protects customers from fraud and unauthorized access
  * Built with industry-standard security practices
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Faster and Smoother Payments" icon="fa-bolt">
  * Optimized checkout experience reduces friction
  * Enables **quick transaction processing**
  * Minimizes drop-offs during payment
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reduced Payment Failures" icon="fa-chart-line">
  * Improves success rates with **real-time transaction handling**
  * Intelligent routing and retry mechanisms help reduce declines
  * Ensures higher payment completion rates
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Multiple Payment Options" icon="fa-money-bill-wave">
  * Accept a wide range of payment methods:
    * Credit & Debit Cards
    * UPI
    * Net Banking
    * Wallets
  * Supports multiple banks and card networks securely
</Accordion>

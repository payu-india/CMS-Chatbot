You can view the settlement details of the transactions routed through PayU aggregator on the PayU Dashboard by navigating to **Settlements** on the left pane.

> 📘 Note:
>
> To view the settlement details of the transactions routed through different aggregators other than PayU, you have to refer to respective aggregators Dashboard.
